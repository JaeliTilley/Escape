ESCAPE - Changes from the one-page design document
====================================================

Kept from the design
--------------------
- 2D top-down haunted house maze, one small house layout, one monster, three keys.
- WASD movement, Space for the flashlight, Enter to start / pause / restart.
- Exit door stays locked until all 3 keys are collected.
- Start screen that explains the controls, game over screen, Enter to restart.
- Walls block the player (physics colliders), and the monster moves around the house and chases the player.

Changes
-------
1. Flashlight battery.
   The design said the flashlight "has to load up." I made this a battery: it drains while Space is held
   and recharges when released. If it runs completely empty, it locks out until it recharges to 35%.
   This keeps the flashlight from being used constantly and makes timing it a real decision.

2. Background music replaced with a gameplay-driven heartbeat.
   The design listed "background creepy music." Instead, the only music-like sound is a heartbeat
   that gets faster and louder the closer the monster is, so the audio tells the player how much
   danger they are in.

3. Hiding works through line of sight.
   Instead of dedicated hiding spots, the monster can only see the player if no wall or furniture
   is in the way. Ducking behind a bookshelf or around a doorway breaks its line of sight, and after
   a few seconds it gives up and searches where it last saw the player. It can still hear the player
   walking if they are very close.

4. Monster gets more dangerous over time.
   Each key collected makes the monster faster while chasing, so the game gets harder as the player
   gets closer to escaping.

5. Monster's eyes glow in the dark.
   The player can only see a small area around themselves, so the monster's red eyes are drawn on top
   of the darkness. This keeps the game fair while staying scary.

6. Scoring system expanded.
   The design said "escape with the highest score possible" without details. Score now comes from:
   keys (+500 each), scaring the monster with the flashlight (+100 each), a time bonus for escaping
   quickly, and a bonus for escaping with battery left. The best score is saved between sessions.

7. Added feedback the design didn't mention.
   - Bumping the locked exit shows how many keys are still missing.
   - The exit door glows green once unlocked.
   - "IT SEES YOU" warning while being chased, screen shake when caught.
   - Keys come in gold, silver, and copper and appear on the HUD as they're collected.
   - Flashlight battery bar on the HUD.

8. Pathfinding.
   The monster uses breadth-first search on the house grid so it moves through doorways and around
   furniture instead of getting stuck on walls, which was one of the challenges listed in the design.
